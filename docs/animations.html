Animated explanations

Five short, narrated-by-motion animations built with Manim — the same engine behind 3Blue1Brown — each unpacking one big idea from the course. Press play, or open the matching interactive page to drive it yourself.

The Central Limit Theorem

A lopsided source distribution on the left; on the right, the distribution of the sample mean tightens into a bell curve as the sample size grows from 1 to 40. → Try the interactive version

The Law of Large Numbers

Four running-average paths of coin flips wander early on, then are all reeled in toward the true mean of 0.5. → Interactive version

Percentiles, Quartiles & the IQR

From a strip of sorted data, the quartiles and median are marked and a boxplot is assembled — with the interquartile range braced underneath. → Interactive version

Joint Distributions & Correlation

A cloud of points rotates from a strong positive correlation to a strong negative one and back to independence as the dial turns from \(\rho=+0.9\) to \(\rho=-0.9\). → Interactive version

The Method of Least Squares

A poorly-placed line swings into position as the sum of its squared residuals (the red sticks) shrinks to the minimum — the least-squares line. → Interactive version

Want to re-render or tweak these? The Manim source for every scene lives in manim/scenes.py in the repository. Each is a small, self-contained Scene class you can edit and re-render with manim -qm scenes.py CLTScene.
