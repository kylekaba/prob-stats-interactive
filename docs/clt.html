The Central Limit Theorem

The CLT says the average of many independent draws is approximately normal — no matter how un-normal the source is. Pick a lopsided source below, raise the sample size, and watch the histogram of sample means snap into a bell curve (Chapter 6).

② The distribution of the sample mean \(\bar X_n\)

What to notice. At \(n=1\) the bottom histogram simply copies the source — skew and all. As \(n\) grows, two things happen together: the histogram becomes symmetric and bell-shaped, and it gets narrower, because the standard error \(\sigma/\sqrt n\) shrinks. The dashed curve is the exact normal the CLT predicts.
