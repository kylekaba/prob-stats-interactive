Probability Distributions

Choose a distribution and drag its parameters. The graph, the mean (dashed line), and the summary statistics all update in real time — the same families introduced in Chapters 3 and 4.

Try this. Switch to the Binomial and slide \(n\) up while keeping \(p\) fixed: the bars pile up into a bell shape — a preview of the Central Limit Theorem. Or set the Poisson mean small and watch how a rare-event count concentrates near zero.
