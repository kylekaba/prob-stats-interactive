Percentiles, the IQR & Exploratory Data Analysis

A single dataset of 240 geyser-eruption durations, viewed three ways. Tune the histogram bins and the kernel bandwidth, then dissect the five-number summary that a boxplot is built from (Chapter 7).

① Histogram & kernel density estimate

The bias–variance tradeoff, by hand. Tiny bins / small bandwidth give a spiky, noisy picture; huge ones smear the two peaks into one. Somewhere in the middle the dataset's bimodal structure appears.

② Percentiles, quartiles & the interquartile range

The shaded band is the interquartile range (IQR), the span of the middle 50% of the data; the moving line is the \(p\)-th empirical quantile \(q_n(p)\).
