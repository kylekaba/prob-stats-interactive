Joint Distributions & Correlation

A bivariate normal in the center, with its two marginals along the edges. Turn the correlation dial and watch the joint density tilt — while the marginals barely change. That gap is the whole lesson of Chapter 5: correlation lives in the joint behavior, not the margins.

Watch the margins. As \(\rho\) swings from \(+0.95\) to \(-0.95\), the cloud rotates from a tight up-slope to a tight down-slope, but the two edge curves (the marginals of \(X\) and \(Y\)) keep the same width. Set \(\rho=0\) and the contours become axis-aligned — \(X\) and \(Y\) are then independent.
