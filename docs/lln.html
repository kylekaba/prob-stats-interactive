The Law of Large Numbers

Each colored path is the running average of one long experiment. Individually they wander, but every one is pulled toward the true mean (the dashed line) as the number of trials grows — that is the Law of Large Numbers (Chapter 6).

Two takeaways. Early on (left side, note the log scale) the averages swing wildly; with few trials, luck dominates. By the right side they have nearly collapsed onto the true mean. The casino option makes the point vivid: the house edge of just \(1/37\) per spin is invisible in a handful of games but inevitable over thousands.
