Least-Squares Regression

Move the line by hand with the slope and intercept sliders. The red sticks are the residuals, and the box keeps a running total of their squared lengths. Your goal: make that total as small as possible — then press Snap to best fit to see how close you got (Chapter 11).

Why squares? Squaring punishes big misses far more than small ones and makes the total a smooth bowl with a single lowest point — the unique least-squares line \(\hat\alpha,\hat\beta\). Notice you can never beat the "best fit" SSE by hand: that is exactly what least squares guarantees.
